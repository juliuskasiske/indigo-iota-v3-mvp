# Nordics Market Entry Brief — June 2026

Nordwind Analytics GmbH. Author: Tomas Ek (Account Executive, Nordics),
reviewed by Sophie Lindqvist. For the H2 planning session, 12 August 2026.

## 1. Where we are

Nordwind opened the Nordics in September 2025 with one account executive based in
Stockholm. Ten months in:

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Closed-won | 2 accounts, 118k ARR (both Swedish) |
| Closed-lost | 4 accounts, 481k ARR |
| Open pipeline | 640k across 7 opportunities |
| Win rate | 33% (Sweden), 0% (Norway and Denmark) |

Every Swedish win and every Norwegian or Danish loss points at the same two
things.

## 2. The two blockers

### 2.1 No Norwegian or Danish interface

The product ships in English and German only. In Sweden this has been tolerable —
the buyers and the operations staff both work comfortably in English. In Norway
and Denmark, terminal and warehouse supervisors do not, and they are the daily
users.

Named in the loss of:
- **Nordfracht Oslo AS** (165k). CEO Kristine Dahl: her supervisors in Oslo and
  Bergen "do not want to work in English". Won by LogiTwin, which ships a
  Norwegian UI.
- **Saltvik Logistikk AS** (120k, still open) — raised at the first workshop.
- **Bergen Kyst AS** (90k, still open) — raised in discovery.

Product estimate for localizing the **operational screens only** (not the full
product): one team, ten weeks, plus approximately 40k of translation and QA.

### 2.2 No local reference customer

Every Norwegian and Danish prospect has asked for a customer in their own market
they can call. We have none, and our two Swedish customers have not agreed to be
named.

Kristine Dahl called two of the three Norwegian references LogiTwin gave her
before deciding. Line Bruun at Vestholm Foods has asked twice.

A reference commitment is an accepted reason for a discount exception under the
Pricing Policy (section 4) and has never once been used for a Nordic deal.

## 3. Competition in the region

| Competitor | Position | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| LogiTwin GmbH | Incumbent challenger | Norwegian and Danish UI, three named Norwegian references, ~35% cheaper | Thin analytics, no forecasting |
| Kestrel Supply IQ | Entering from the US | SAP and Dynamics connectors, deep pockets | Also English-only; no Nordic references |

LogiTwin beats us on the two things we lack and loses to us on the thing we are
best at. That is a solvable competitive position, not a structural one.

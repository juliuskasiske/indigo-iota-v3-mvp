# Win/Loss Analysis — H1 2026

Nordwind Analytics GmbH. Compiled by Sales Ops from CRM loss reasons plus 14
post-decision interviews. Owner: Lena Brandt.

Scope: 80 decided opportunities in H1 2026 (23 won, 57 lost).

## 1. Why we lost — 57 losses

| Primary reason | Deals | ARR lost | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price / competitor undercut | 17 | 1.42M | 30% |
| Missing integration (SAP S/4HANA) | 9 | 0.78M | 16% |
| Procurement / security review timed out | 7 | 0.61M | 12% |
| No local language or local reference (Nordics) | 6 | 0.55M | 11% |
| Lost to "do nothing" / no budget | 11 | 0.74M | 19% |
| Product capability gap | 4 | 0.21M | 7% |
| Other | 3 | 0.14M | 5% |

Only 7% of losses were decided on product capability. 58% were decided on
commercial terms, integrations, procurement friction, or localization — none of
which an account executive can influence in the deal.

## 2. Competitors named

| Competitor | Times in a lost deal | Typical price position | What they lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Supply IQ (US) | 21 | ~15% below our quote | Certified SAP and Dynamics connectors; 20% partner referral fee |
| LogiTwin GmbH | 9 | ~35% below our quote | Norwegian and Danish UI; local references; lightweight |
| In-house / spreadsheets | 11 | — | No procurement process |

Kestrel appears in 21 of 57 losses and won 14 of them. In the seven Kestrel deals
we won, our average discount was 24%.

## 3. The four losses worth reading in full

### Steinbeck Automotive GmbH — 120k, lost 23 June
Kestrel quoted 96k against our 120k. Decision-maker Uwe Bartsch (Head of
Logistics) named two reasons: price, and the certified S/4HANA connector. We
proposed a middleware layer their IT team would have had to own. We had already
discounted 15% and did not offer more, because a further exception would have
been the fourth of the quarter.

### Nordfracht Oslo AS — 165k, lost 12 May
CEO Kristine Dahl: the product is English-only and terminal supervisors in Oslo
and Bergen will not work in English; and she asked for a reference customer in
Norway or Denmark and we had none. LogiTwin supplied three Norwegian references,
two of which she called. She explicitly invited us to return once localized.

### Kranz Pharma GmbH — 140k, not lost, stalled 8+ weeks
Commercially approved by procurement in April. Blocked entirely on a vendor
security assessment: ISO 27001 required by group policy, a 214-question
questionnaire answered by hand, and a DPA needing a sub-processor annex we did
not have written down. Budget is committed for this fiscal year only.

### Hansa Chemie — 210k, never reached first proposal
Disqualified themselves on the first call when we confirmed there is no certified
SAP connector.

The three deals won on "speed of implementation promised" are worth noting
against the Customer Health Report: two of them are now in the at-risk list,
because the implementation promise made during the sale was not costed with
Customer Success and was not met.

## 5. Patterns Sales Ops would put in front of leadership

1. **Kestrel's 15% price gap is being answered with discounting rather than
   differentiation.** Six of fourteen H1 discount exceptions cite a competitor;
   eight cite nothing.
2. **One missing integration explains 16% of lost ARR** (0.78M) and disqualified
   a further 210k before the first proposal.
3. **Procurement friction is a company-level cost, not a deal-level one.** Seven
   deals timed out in security review; the same 214-question exercise was
   repeated four times by the same solutions engineer.
4. **The Nordics losses are all one story**: no localized UI, no local reference.
   The same two questions will be asked of 640k of open Nordics pipeline.
5. **We win referral deals.** Blaubaum-sourced deals close in 61 days against a
   94-day average and at a maximum 10% discount — and we have never paid Blaubaum
   anything.

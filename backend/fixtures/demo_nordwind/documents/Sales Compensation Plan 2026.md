# Sales Compensation Plan 2026 — Nordwind Analytics GmbH

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Ralf Neumann (CFO), approved by Bernd Kolthoff.
Applies to Account Executives, SDRs and the VP Sales.

## 1. Account Executive plan

| Component | Value |
|---|---|
| Base salary | 78,000–94,500 depending on band |
| On-target variable | 62,000 |
| On-target earnings | 140,000–156,500 |
| Annual quota | 1,050,000 new ARR |

### Commission rates

| Revenue type | Rate | Counts toward quota? |
|---|---|---|
| New logo ARR | **10.0%** | Yes, at 100% |
| Expansion ARR in an existing account | **3.0%** | Yes, at 50% |
| Renewal (flat) | 0% | No |
| Renewal with uplift | 3.0% on the uplift only | At 50% |
| Professional services | 0% | No |

### Accelerators

| Attainment | Multiplier on new logo ARR |
|---|---|
| 0–80% | 1.0x |
| 80–100% | 1.25x |
| 100–130% | 1.6x |
| >130% | 2.0x |

Accelerators apply to **new logo ARR only**. Expansion ARR is excluded from the
accelerator base.

## 2. The incentive this creates

Worked example, using two live Q3 opportunities:

| | Hafenlogistik expansion | Vestholm Foods (new logo) |
|---|---|---|
| ARR | 180,000 | 240,000 |
| Rate | 3.0% | 10.0% |
| Quota credit | 90,000 (50%) | 240,000 (100%) |
| Commission at 1.0x | 5,400 | 24,000 |
| Commission at 1.6x accelerator | 5,400 (excluded) | 38,400 |

An account executive holding both deals earns between 4.4x and 7.1x more for the
same effort on the new logo. The expansion is also the higher-probability deal —
it was initiated by the customer.

## 3. SDR plan

| Component | Value |
|---|---|
| Base | 46,000 |
| Variable | 18,000 |
| Metric | Qualified opportunities accepted by an AE |
| Target | 34 per quarter |

There is no component for speed of response, and no penalty for a lead that is
never worked. Team size: 3 SDRs against roughly 200 inbound MQLs each per
quarter, plus their own outbound list building.

## 4. What this plan does not pay for

1. **Expansion revenue**, at 3% against 10%, excluded from accelerators, and at
   half quota credit — despite expansion being the company's lowest-CAC revenue
   (NRR 96%, and see the Customer Health Report on time-to-value).
2. **Retention.** No AE component is tied to whether the customer renews, so
   implementation promises made in the sale carry no cost to the person making
   them.
3. **Partner-sourced revenue.** There is no partner referral fee schedule and no
   split defined, so no one at Nordwind owns the partner relationship.
4. **Speed of first response** on inbound leads, which the SDR data shows is the
   strongest single predictor of MQL-to-opportunity conversion (14% inside 24
   hours vs 3% after five days).
5. **Discount discipline.** Commission is paid on booked ARR regardless of
   discount, so a 25% discount costs the company margin and costs the seller
   nothing beyond the smaller ARR.

# Q2 2026 Pipeline Review — Nordwind Analytics GmbH

Prepared by Sales Ops for the leadership review, 30 June 2026.
Owner: Lena Brandt (VP Sales). All figures in EUR, ARR basis.

## 1. Headline

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q1 2026 | Q4 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| New ARR closed | 1.31M | 1.42M | 1.68M |
| Target | 1.75M | 1.75M | 1.60M |
| Attainment | 75% | 81% | 105% |
| Deals closed-won | 12 | 11 | 16 |
| Deals closed-lost | 41 | 39 | 35 |
| Win rate | 23% | 22% | 31% |
| Average ACV | 78k | 78k | 74k |
| Average sales cycle (days) | 96 | 94 | 81 |
| Average discount granted | 19.4% | 18.8% | 11.2% |
| Total ARR | 6.20M | 5.94M | 5.61M |

Win rate has fallen 8 points in two quarters while demo-to-proposal conversion
has held at 64%. The loss is happening after the proposal, not in the product
evaluation.

target is **2.2x**. At a 23% win rate, healthy coverage would be 3.5x.

## 4. Cycle time

| Deal type | Median cycle |
|---|---|
| No vendor security review | 94 days |
| With vendor security review | 148 days |
| Sourced by Blaubaum Consulting (referral) | 61 days |

Referral-sourced deals are both the fastest and the least discounted (max
discount observed: 10%). There is no partner agreement and no referral fee in
place.

## 5. Stage conversion

| Stage → next | Q2 | Q4 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| MQL → qualified opportunity | 8% | 13% |
| Discovery → demo | 71% | 69% |
| Demo → proposal | 64% | 64% |
| Proposal → closed-won | 31% | 44% |

MQL → opportunity conversion halved. 38% of Q2 MQLs had no first touch inside
five business days (SDR report, Jonas Weiss).

   implemented in the quote tool.

3. Decide the roadmap trade-off (SAP connector vs Nordic localization) — six
   opportunities worth 770k are behind one or the other.
4. Put a referral fee and an owner on the Blaubaum relationship.
5. Set and instrument a four-hour first-response SLA on inbound MQLs.

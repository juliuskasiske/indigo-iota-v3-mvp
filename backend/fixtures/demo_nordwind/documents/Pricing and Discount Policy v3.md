# Pricing and Discount Policy v3 — Nordwind Analytics GmbH

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Ralf Neumann (CFO). Supersedes v2 (2025).

## 1. List pricing — Nordwind Signal

| Edition | Price per named user / year | Minimum seats | Typical customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal Core | 1,180 | 10 | Single site, up to 50 employees |
| Signal Professional | 1,640 | 25 | Multi-site mid-market |
| Signal Enterprise | 2,150 | 50 | Multi-terminal / multi-plant |

Platform fee: 18,000 per year (Professional), 34,000 per year (Enterprise).
Implementation: quoted separately, minimum 12,000.

All pricing is **per named user**. There is currently no consumption-based or
per-shipment tier. Seat sharing is prohibited under clause 6.2 of the MSA.

## 2. Term and payment

Standard term is 12 months, annual payment in advance. 3-year terms attract a
standing 8% discount and are the preferred structure.

## 3. Gross margin targets

| Line | Target GM |
|---|---|
| Subscription | 84% |
| Implementation | 42% |
| Blended | 78% |

Observed blended GM per customer in H1 2026 ranged from **61% to 88%**. The
variance is not commercial: it tracks how far a customer's actual shipment volume
has drifted from their seat count. Cost to serve scales with shipments processed;
revenue scales with seats bought.

## 4. Discount authority

| Discount | Approval required | When |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12% | Account Executive | — |
| 12–20% | VP Sales, in writing, with a stated reason | **before** the quote is issued |
| >20% | VP Sales **and** CFO | **before** the quote is issued |

Reasons accepted for an exception: competitive displacement with a named
competitor and quote; multi-year commitment of 3 years or more; a reference
commitment written into the order form.

**Not** accepted: "to close this quarter", "customer asked", "the deal was going
cold".

## 6. Known gaps in this policy

1. The approval gate in section 4 is not enforced anywhere in the quote tool. It
   is an email convention, and the log above shows it is honoured after the fact
   or not at all.
2. There is no consumption or per-shipment tier, although seasonal customers
   (food, agriculture, retail logistics) routinely ask for one. Nine of 34
   customers show login patterns consistent with seat sharing.
3. There is no partner or referral pricing schedule of any kind.
4. Expansion pricing is not defined: expansions are quoted at list and discounted
   ad hoc, despite being the lowest-cost revenue the company has.

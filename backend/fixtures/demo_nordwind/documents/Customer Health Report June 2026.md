# Customer Health Report — June 2026

Nordwind Analytics GmbH. Owner: Katrin Sölle (Head of Customer Success).
Covers the 34 active customers as at 30 June 2026.

## 1. Retention

| Metric | H1 2026 | H2 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Net revenue retention | 96% | 101% |
| Gross revenue churn | 11% | 7% |
| Expansion revenue | 7% | 8% |
| Logo churn | 2 of 34 | 1 of 31 |
| Churned ARR | 71k | 38k |

Both H1 churns were SMB accounts, and both gave the same reason at the exit
conversation: *"we never got it fully working"*.

## 2. Time to first value

Median across all accounts: **71 days**.

| Cohort | Accounts | Median time to first value | Renewed / on track |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Success engaged **before** signature | 2 | 28 days | 2 of 2 |
| Customer Success engaged **after** signature | 7 | 79 days | 4 of 7 |

Of nine accounts onboarded since January, CS was involved before signature in
**two**. Time to first value is the strongest predictor of renewal in our data.

## 4. Support load

| Segment | Customers | Share of ARR | Share of tickets | Tickets per €10k ARR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | 9 | 68% | 31% | 0.9 |
| Mid-market | 14 | 23% | 34% | 2.1 |

SMB customers generate 2.3 times the tickets per euro of ARR that enterprise
customers do, while contributing 9% of ARR.

## 6. What Customer Success is asking for

1. CS in the room **before** signature on every deal above 50k. The two accounts
   where this happened reached value in 28 days instead of 79.
2. Implementation commitments made during a sales cycle to be written into the
   order form and costed with CS before the quote goes out. Alpenmetall's "four
   weeks" was never written down anywhere.
3. A defined expansion motion with an owner. Right now expansion happens when a
   customer emails us, and then waits.
